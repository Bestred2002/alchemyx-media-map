## 3. HOMEPAGE EN — FAQ SECTION

### H2
```
Frequently asked questions about Alchemyx
```

### FAQ 1
**Q**: What is Alchemyx?
**A**: Alchemyx is the first Italian self-serve AI platform that enables SMBs to plan and run multichannel advertising campaigns combining radio, television, cinema, DOOH, social media and Google Search, with budgets starting from €2,000 and no advertising agency required. It is developed by Alchemyst LAB Srl, an innovative startup based in Milan, Italy.

### FAQ 2
**Q**: How much does Alchemyx cost?
**A**: Alchemyx charges a transparent commission of 13.98% on the advertising budget invested. There are no fixed costs or subscriptions in the basic plan. The minimum budget to start a campaign is €2,000. A Premium plan with advanced reporting is available with an annual subscription.

### FAQ 3
**Q**: What advertising channels does Alchemyx support?
**A**: Alchemyx supports radio (local and national), addressable television and CTV (connected TV), cinema advertising, DOOH (digital out-of-home billboards), social media (Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn) and Google (Search and Display). All channels are manageable from a single interface.

### FAQ 4
**Q**: Is Alchemyx available outside Italy?
**A**: Currently, Alchemyx is exclusively available for the Italian market. Commercial launch in Italy is planned for Q1 2027. International expansion is planned for Spain (2028), France and Germany (2029–2030), and the United States (2031).

### FAQ 5
**Q**: When will Alchemyx launch?
**A**: The MVP will be ready in July 2026, followed by a pilot program in September 2026 with a selected group of SMBs and agency partners. The official commercial launch on the Italian market is planned for Q1 2027.

### FAQ 6
**Q**: How is Alchemyx different from Mediaset AdManager or Sky Advertising Manager?
**A**: Mediaset AdManager and Sky Advertising Manager only provide advertising on their respective networks. Alchemyx is network-independent and aggregates all channels — radio, cinema, DOOH, social, and search — in one platform, regardless of publisher or broadcaster.

### FAQ 7
**Q**: Is Alchemyx the same as Alchemyst AI?
**A**: No. Alchemyst AI is a different company based in Bengaluru, India, focused on sales AI agents. Alchemyx (alchemyx.ai) is an Italian advertising technology platform developed by Alchemyst LAB Srl, Milan. They are entirely separate products with no relationship.
